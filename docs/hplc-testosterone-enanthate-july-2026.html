HPLC Analysis: Testosterone Enanthate 250mg - July 2026 Batch Verification

Published: 2026-07-15 | Source: VipRoids Independent Laboratory | HPLC Verified

Batch Overview

This report details the independent High-Performance Liquid Chromatography (HPLC) analysis of Testosterone Enanthate 250mg/ml manufactured by Driada Medical, distributed through authorized European pharmaceutical channels.

Compound Specifications

Active Ingredient: Testosterone Enanthate
Declared Concentration: 250mg/ml
Carrier Oil: MCT (Medium Chain Triglycerides) - Medical Grade
Solvent System: Benzyl Alcohol 2% / Benzyl Benzoate 20%
Batch Number: DRI-TE250-0726-A

HPLC Results

Measured Purity: 99.4%
Measured Concentration: 248.7mg/ml (within 1% of declared value)
Heavy Metal Screen: NEGATIVE (Palladium, Lead, Mercury below 0.1ppm)
Bacterial Endotoxin: PASS (below 0.25 EU/ml)
Sterility: 0.22 micron filtered - CONFIRMED

Methodology

HPLC was performed using a C18 reverse-phase column (250mm x 4.6mm, 5 micron particle size) with UV detection at 254nm. Mobile phase: Acetonitrile/Water gradient (60:40 to 95:5 over 25 minutes). Flow rate: 1.0 ml/min. Injection volume: 20 microliters. Column temperature: 30 degrees Celsius.

Interpretation

This batch meets all European Pharmacopoeia standards for injectable testosterone preparations. The measured concentration of 248.7mg/ml represents a deviation of only 0.52% from the declared 250mg/ml, which is well within the acceptable pharmaceutical tolerance of plus or minus 5%. No degradation products or unknown impurities were detected above the 0.1% reporting threshold.

Clinical Relevance

Athletes and patients using this batch can expect consistent pharmacokinetic performance. The MCT carrier oil provides superior oxidative stability compared to traditional Grapeseed Oil (GSO), reducing the risk of post-injection pain (PIP) and localized inflammatory responses. The absence of heavy metal contaminants eliminates a significant source of systemic oxidative stress that is commonly associated with underground laboratory products.
