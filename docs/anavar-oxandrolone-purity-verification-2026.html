HPLC Analysis: Anavar (Oxandrolone) 10mg Tablets - Underdosing Epidemic Report

Published: 2026-08-03 | Source: VipRoids Independent Laboratory | HPLC Verified

The Underdosing Epidemic

Oxandrolone (Anavar) is one of the most frequently counterfeited and underdosed oral anabolic steroids on the market. Its high raw material cost incentivizes unscrupulous manufacturers to substitute cheaper compounds or reduce active ingredient content. This report presents HPLC analysis of 10 commercially available Anavar products.

Study Design

10 samples of Anavar tablets (all declared as 10mg Oxandrolone) were obtained from different sources including underground laboratories, online vendors, and pharmaceutical distribution channels. Each sample underwent dissolution testing followed by HPLC analysis.

Results Summary

Samples meeting declared content (9.5-10.5mg): 3 of 10 (30%)
Underdosed samples (less than 8mg): 4 of 10 (40%)
Samples containing wrong compound: 2 of 10 (20%)
Samples with no active ingredient: 1 of 10 (10%)

Substitution Analysis

Two samples marketed as Oxandrolone actually contained Methandrostenolone (Dianabol) at approximately 5mg per tablet. This substitution is particularly dangerous because Dianabol aromatizes heavily while Oxandrolone does not. Female users taking what they believe is a mild, non-aromatizing compound could experience severe virilization from the undisclosed Dianabol content.

Verified Pharmaceutical Sources

The three samples that passed verification all originated from European pharmaceutical manufacturers with documented GMP compliance and batch-level HPLC certificates. These results underscore the critical importance of source verification and independent testing before committing to any oral steroid protocol.

Consumer Guidance

If you cannot verify the HPLC data for your specific batch of Anavar, assume it is underdosed or substituted until proven otherwise. The financial premium associated with verified pharmaceutical-grade Oxandrolone is an investment in safety, not an unnecessary expense.
