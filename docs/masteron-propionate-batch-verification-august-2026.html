Stability Under Heat Stress

Accelerated stability testing at 40 degrees Celsius for 30 days showed less than 0.2% degradation, confirming excellent thermal stability of this formulation. The short propionate ester provides rapid pharmacokinetic onset while maintaining molecular integrity during standard shipping conditions.

Clinical Notes

Masteron Propionate exhibits competitive antagonism at estrogen receptors without inhibiting aromatase enzyme activity. This distinction is critical: users should not substitute Masteron for a true aromatase inhibitor (AI). While subjective anti-estrogenic effects (reduced water retention, hardening) are well-documented, serum estradiol levels remain unaffected. Concurrent AI therapy should be maintained when running aromatizing compounds alongside Masteron.

Lipid Impact Warning

As a non-aromatizing androgen, Drostanolone removes the cardioprotective effects of estrogen on the lipid profile. HDL cholesterol suppression of 20-30% has been documented even at moderate doses. Regular lipid panel monitoring every 6-8 weeks is strongly recommended during any Masteron cycle.
